# Mechanic narration

19 English narration clips generated locally with the installed macOS Samantha
synthetic voice at 165 words per minute. These are synthetic speech, not recorded
voice acting. Exact scripts are in Narration.json. POINTS maps to +PTS and HAZARD
maps to !. Other filenames match the tutorial mechanic keys.

TutorialVoice plays these bundled clips with the game's master/SFX volume and
SFX mixer routing. Animated lesson Replay restarts speech; Pause pauses it;
Skip/close stops it before the next lesson. No speech service or network is
required during gameplay. Clips finish before the 14-second demonstration ends.

Validate with RealBuca → Toy Box 3D → Validate Narrated Mechanic Tutorials.
This checks clip presence/duration and tutorial selection across all 30 saved
levels, including multi-mechanic levels, restarting, direct entry into a later
level, and filtering previously seen lessons. Player preferences are restored.

Tutorials now show on every level entry, including repeat selections and restarts.
Previously watched preferences do not suppress the honey picture or mechanic lessons.
