# Grand train welcome voices

YouDidIt.wav and WelcomeWorldTwo.wav were synthesized locally using the macOS Junior voice (`say`), then converted to PCM WAV with `afconvert`. They are synthetic character dialogue, not recordings of children.

Dialogue: “You did it! Here is your ticket!” and “Amazing! Welcome to world two!”
The sequence also uses the existing HappyYeah cheer and StationDeparture effect; their existing provenance remains unchanged.
